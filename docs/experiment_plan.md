# Experiment Plan

## Experiment 1 Baseline Scheduling

Compare earliest due date, shortest processing time, and first-in-first-out dispatching rules without proactive maintenance.

## Experiment 2 Maintenance Policy Comparison

Compare corrective maintenance, preventive maintenance, and condition-based maintenance under the same order set.

## Experiment 3 Sensitivity Analysis

Change degradation rate, maintenance duration, due-date tightness, and workstation capacity to observe robustness.

## Experiment 4 MARL Extension

Use each workstation or equipment unit as an agent. Define local observations, maintenance and dispatching actions, and rewards based on delay, utilization, and maintenance cost.

## Reporting

Export metrics tables to `paper_materials/tables` and timeline or comparison figures to `paper_materials/figures`.
