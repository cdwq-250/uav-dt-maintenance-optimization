# Thesis Framework

## Chapter 1 Introduction

Introduce UAV manufacturing and maintenance pressure, the value of digital twins, and the research significance of maintenance-aware scheduling.

## Chapter 2 Related Work

Review production scheduling, equipment maintenance optimization, digital-twin modeling, and reinforcement learning applications in workshop decision-making.

## Chapter 3 Problem Description and Model Assumptions

Define orders, processes, workstations, equipment degradation, maintenance policies, and optimization objectives.

## Chapter 4 Digital-Twin Simulation Model

Describe the simulation architecture, data flow, entity classes, state update rules, and performance metrics.

## Chapter 5 Maintenance Optimization Experiments

Compare baseline scheduling, corrective maintenance, preventive maintenance, and condition-based maintenance. Add sensitivity analysis for degradation rate, repair time, and due-date tightness.

## Chapter 6 Conclusion and Future Work

Summarize findings and discuss future work such as real sensor integration, parameter calibration, and multi-agent reinforcement learning.
